# Problem statement (agreed version)

Retail investors who follow a handful of Indian stocks have to piece together each results update from exchange filings, broker alerts and news. It takes a long time to work out what changed and whether it matters, and many don't verify against the filing at all, so they act on someone else's summary. FINN tells them which update matters, what changed, and shows the exact line in the source filing, so they can understand and check it in minutes.
