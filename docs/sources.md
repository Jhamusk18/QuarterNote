## Market size

1. NSE unique registered investors: 13.24 crore (132.4 million), as of 30 June 2026.
   Source: BusinessToday, "NSE IPO: 26 crore investor accounts and 3,000+ listed companies — 10 numbers to know", 17 Sep 2026.
   https://www.businesstoday.in/markets/ipo-corner/story/nse-ipo-26-crore-investor-accounts-and-3000-listed-companies-10-numbers-to-know-555977-2026-09-17

2. NSE unique trading accounts (UCCs): 26.14 crore, as of 30 June 2026. Higher than (1) because one
   investor can hold accounts with more than one broker.
   Source: same as above.

3. New investors added in FY26 (Apr 2025-Mar 2026): about 2.1 crore, 23.2% growth in registrations.
   Source: ANI / Tribune India, "Despite moderation in recent months, stock markets added 2.1 cr
   new investors in FY26: NSE Report", 1 May 2026.
   https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/despite-moderation-in-recent-months-stock-markets-added-2-1-cr-new-investors-in-fy26-nse-report

4. New SIP accounts opened, Apr 2025-Mar 2026: 7.2 crore.
   Source: NSE press release, 7 June 2026 ("Investor accounts cross the milestone of 26-crore").
   https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/web/pressrelease/2026-06/PR_cc_07062026_20260610124631.pdf

5. Individual investors' direct + mutual-fund ownership of NSE-listed market cap: 18.7%, as of
   31 Mar 2026.
   Source: India CSR, "NSE Crosses 26 Crore Investor Accounts as India's Equity Culture Goes
   Mainstream", 7 June 2026.
   https://indiacsr.in/nse-crosses-26-crore-investor-accounts-india-equity-culture/

6. Geographic spread: Maharashtra leads with ~2 crore investors, followed by Uttar Pradesh (1.5 crore)
   and Gujarat (1.1 crore), as of 27 Apr 2026.
   Source: Business Today, "NSE's 13-crore milestone...", 27 Apr 2026.
   https://www.businesstoday.in/markets/story/nses-13-crore-milestone-sip-boom-digital-access-fuel-investor-participation-hunger-527685-2026-04-27
