Problem: retail investors following a handful of Indian stocks have to piece together each announcement from exchange PDFs, broker alerts, Screener and secondhand summaries. Evidence: QuarterNote’s live NSE pull for 18 companies, 29 Oct to 27 Nov 2026, returned 210 filings, of which 35 were classified high priority. That is about 117 filings a month for a 10-stock watchlist, roughly 1 in 6 material. ITC’s own Q1 FY27 filing warns that its revenue lines are not strictly comparable year on year, which shows why a headline number can mislead. Market sources: NSE unique registered investors 13.24 crore as of 30 June 2026 (Business Today, 17 Sep 2026). About 2.1 crore new investors in FY26 (ANI / Tribune India, 1 May 2026). 7.2 crore new SIP accounts in FY26 (NSE press release, 7 June 2026). Individuals own 18.7% of NSE-listed market value via direct and mutual fund holdings, 31 Mar 2026 (India CSR, 7 June 2026). SAM and SOM are my planning assumptions. Design choices: lead with one finding and a size-of-change meter; at most three key points; the chart matches the investor’s question; a Still unknown section says when each gap will close; long documents are read in the background. Deliberately left out: buy or sell calls, price targets, social features and trading, to stay on the research side of SEBI rules and keep the product focused. Competitor notes are judgment from using each product, not measured data.
