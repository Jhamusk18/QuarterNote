Problem statement: when a company I follow makes an announcement, I want to understand what changed and find the evidence quickly. Today the investor opens a long exchange PDF, finds the table, reads the notes, compares periods and checks the news. Many skip this and rely on someone else’s summary. User: a self-directed Indian equity investor with 5 to 15 NSE stocks who checks on a phone and has no time to read full filings. Proof the work is real: ITC’s Q1 FY27 results filing (NSE, 31 Jul 2026) says revenue and excise lines are not strictly comparable with earlier periods, so a headline growth number can mislead. Tata Steel disclosed its APL Apollo Tubes deal and the completion of the first tranche in separate NSE filings on 23 Nov 2026; later steps still need approvals. Today vs QuarterNote: six manual steps against three taps. The time saving is a hypothesis; the first test measures time to a correct, source-backed answer. Prototype: quarternote.onrender.com. ITC shows each filing row as printed with its page. Tata Steel shows which steps are completed and which are still conditional. The layout changes by event because the investor’s question changes. The click-to-verify interaction came from the clickable KPI tree I built during my internship. Trading and recommendations are left out to stay on the research side of SEBI rules. Market: 13.24 crore unique NSE-registered investors as of 30 June 2026 (Business Today, 17 Sep 2026) is a broad audience proxy. SAM assumes 5% actively track individual stocks: 66.2 lakh. Year-one SOM: 4 results seasons × 4,000 visits × 25% activation = 4,000 activated users; 5% pay = 200. Competitors: Screener.in compares quarters but does not explain an announcement; NSE and BSE give the original PDF without guidance; Quartr covers earnings calls, not the filed figures. This is my judgment from using each tool.
