The question: when a company I follow makes an announcement, what changed, and where can I verify it? Whether it matters to a decision depends on the investor, so QuarterNote explains why an update was flagged rather than telling anyone what to do. Evidence of the work: ITC’s Q1 FY27 results filing (NSE, 31 Jul 2026) notes that revenue lines are not strictly comparable with earlier periods because of a sharp excise duty change, so a headline growth number can mislead. Tata Steel disclosed its APL Apollo Tubes deal and the completion of the first tranche in separate NSE filings on 23 Nov 2026; tranches 2 and 3 and the open offer still depend on approvals. How often investors struggle with this is a hypothesis the first test will check. Demo: in the app, open the ITC update and tap View filing row to see every row as printed in the PDF, its page number and how operating profit is calculated from verified rows. Open the Tata Steel update to see the completed first tranche separated from the conditional later steps, each with its page. Each update type gets its own explanation: results need comparable figures; a deal needs status and conditions; a tax order needs the amount and next step if disclosed. In the verified examples, reported figures link to the filing, calculations show their inputs, and unverified figures are marked. Market: 13.24 crore unique NSE-registered investors as of 30 June 2026 is a broad audience proxy (Business Today, 17 Sep 2026). About 2.1 crore new investors joined in FY26 (ANI / Tribune India, 1 May 2026). SAM assumes 5% actively track individual stocks.
