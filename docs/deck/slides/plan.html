Acquisition: launch around the January 2027 results season. Funnel: 10 community posts × 120 visits, 5 newsletters × 400 visits and 800 referral visits give about 4,000 qualified visits; at 25% activation that is 1,000 activated users. An activated user opens a material update for a company they follow. Cash budget ₹25,000, so about ₹25 per activated user, excluding founder time. Pricing logic: the free tier must deliver the core promise (what changed and where to check) so trust builds; Pro charges for depth and breadth that cost us more: a bigger watchlist, AI research questions and guidance tracking over time. ₹199 a month sits below typical premium research subscriptions, which is my judgment, not measured data. Unit economics: ₹199 net revenue minus about ₹50 variable cost for AI and data gives ₹149 contribution. A ₹500 paid acquisition cost pays back in about 3.4 months. At 6% monthly churn, lifetime is about 16.7 months, so LTV ≈ ₹2,480 and LTV:CAC ≈ 5. All are planning assumptions. Variable cost excludes fixed data licences. Because a filing is read once and reused for everyone who follows that company, AI cost per user falls as users per company grow. Phases: 1) free pilot through one results season to prove repeat use; 2) Pro test once week-4 retention holds; 3) license QuarterNote reads to brokers and wealth platforms per user. Risks: QuarterNote states impact and evidence but never recommends. If opinion features are ever added, SEBI Research Analyst registration must be assessed first. India’s DPDP Act 2023 sets consent and deletion duties. Commercial use of exchange data needs the right licence. Accuracy: numbers come from fixed formulas, not the model, and every figure is labelled and linked. Success: the north star measures understanding with verification, not just opens. Kill or pivot if week-4 retention stays below 15% after one season.
