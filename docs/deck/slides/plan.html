Acquisition: launch around the January 2027 results season, when the pain peaks. Funnel: 10 community posts × 120 visits, 5 newsletters × 400 visits and 800 referral visits give about 4,000 qualified visits. At 25% activation that is 1,000 activated users; at 30% week-4 retention, 300 stay; at 5% conversion, about 50 pay. An activated user opens a material update for a company they follow. Cash budget ₹25,000, so about ₹25 per activated user, excluding founder time. Pricing logic: the free tier delivers the core promise (what changed and where to check) so trust builds. Pro charges for depth and breadth that cost more to serve: unlimited watchlist, AI research questions and guidance tracking over time. Unit economics: ₹199 net revenue minus about ₹50 variable cost for AI and data gives ₹149 contribution. A ₹500 paid acquisition cost pays back in about 3.4 months. At 6% monthly churn, lifetime is about 16.7 months, so LTV ≈ ₹2,480 and LTV:CAC ≈ 5. All are planning assumptions; variable cost excludes fixed data licences. A filing is read once and reused for everyone who follows that company, so AI cost per user falls as users per company grow. Roadmap gates: move from the free pilot to Pro only if week-4 retention reaches 30%; move to broker licensing only if 5% pay and payback stays under 6 months. Kill or pivot if week-4 retention stays below 15% after one season. Risks: QuarterNote states impact and evidence but never recommends; SEBI Research Analyst registration must be assessed before any opinion feature. India’s DPDP Act 2023 sets consent and deletion duties: no sign-up, watchlist on device, uploads private and deletable. Commercial use of exchange data needs an NSE licence before charging. Accuracy: numbers come from fixed formulas, not the model; every figure is labelled and linked, with monthly audits targeting 98% accuracy. Success: the north star measures understanding with verification, not just opens. First test: 8 investors answer the same question using NSE alone and then QuarterNote, timed.
