Goal: test repeat use first, then test pricing. A January 2027 results season pilot is proposed. One season shows whether people use the app during earnings week; a habit is only claimed if they return for the next reporting cycle. Acquisition (planning estimates): about 2,000 visits from 5 newsletters, 1,200 from investor communities and 800 from sharing and referrals give 4,000 qualified visits. 4,000 × 25% activation = 1,000 activated users, where activated means reviewed one material update for a company they follow. ₹25,000 ÷ 1,000 = ₹25 per activated user. With 50 paying users, the same spend is ₹500 per paying user. Pricing (to be tested): the basic update and its evidence stay free. Pro at an assumed ₹199 a month adds larger watchlists and deeper research; “unlimited” is avoided until heavy-usage cost is known. ₹199 assumed net monthly revenue − ₹50 assumed variable cost = ₹149 contribution. ₹500 ÷ ₹149 ≈ 3.4 months simple payback. No lifetime value is claimed because paid retention has not been measured. Success: north star is weekly users who review a material update for a company they follow. Test target: reach a correct, source-backed answer in under 2 minutes. Method: give eight target users comparable tasks in QuarterNote and in their usual tools, alternating which tool comes first. Source opens show that someone opened the evidence; the usability test shows whether they understood it; accuracy audits check figure, period, units and reporting basis against the cited row. Launch requirements: have someone qualified review applicable SEBI research and advice rules, privacy obligations and exchange data rights before public distribution or commercial use, as applicable. Make uploads private and deletable before launch. Broker licensing is a possible later option and is not part of this plan.
