# Outline: the parts a reviewer looks for

| Part | Your answer |
|---|---|
| User | An active retail investor who owns or tracks 5 to 15 NSE/BSE stocks and reads quarterly results (give them a name and city, for example "Ankit, 31, Pune, software engineer, follows 10 stocks") |
| Job to be done | "When a company I follow reports results, help me quickly know what changed and whether to look deeper" |
| Current workaround | Open the exchange site or a broker alert, then a screener site, then news, then maybe the PDF |
| Pain | Time per company, missed filings, secondhand summaries that can't be verified |
| Why now | The investor base keeps growing: NSE reported 13.24 crore unique registered investors as of 30 June 2026, and about 2.1 crore were added in FY26. More new investors means more people following results without a research habit or a team |
| Hypothesis | They would use a tool that shows what changed with the evidence one tap away, instead of a summary they have to trust |
